## 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a **PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Duke University** and a **Graduate Research Assistant in the Kamaleswaran Lab**, where I develop AI methods for pediatric critical care.

My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning, clinical data, representation learning, retrieval, and foundation models**, with the goal of developing trustworthy AI systems that can support real-world clinical decision-making.

- 🎓 PhD Student in **Electrical and Computer Engineering** at **Duke University**
- 🔬 Graduate Research Assistant at the **Kamaleswaran Lab**
- 🏥 Building AI systems for **pediatric critical care and clinical decision support**
- 🧠 Working on **representation learning, retrieval methods, foundation models, and large language models**
- 📊 Interested in learning from **EHR, physiological time series, clinical text, imaging, and multimodal clinical data**
- 🎓 BSc in **Computer Science and Engineering** from **North South University**
- 👩‍🏫 Previously served as both an **Undergraduate and Graduate Teaching Assistant**
- 💼 Former **Software Engineering Intern at Cloudly**
- 🤝 Open to research collaborations in **Healthcare AI, Multimodal Learning, Medical Imaging, Clinical Foundation Models, and Trustworthy Machine Learning**
- 💻 Explore my work on **[GitHub](https://github.com/TasnubaTiha33?tab=repositories)**
- 🌐 Visit my **[Personal Website](https://tasnuba-portfolio.vercel.app)**
- 📫 Reach me at **tasnubatiha12@gmail.com**

### 🩻 LightSeg — Fetal Ultrasound Segmentation

Lightweight fetal ultrasound segmentation framework incorporating deep learning, hybrid loss functions, knowledge distillation, and explainability.

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_on_GitHub-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/TasnubaTiha33/Lightseg-A-Fetal-Head-Segmentation-Approach)

---

### 🎙️ Speech Emotion Recognition
